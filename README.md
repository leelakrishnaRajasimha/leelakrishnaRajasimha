## Some projects I am proud of

**[URLX](https://github.com/leelakrishnaRajasimha/URLX)** — A secure URL shortener API with JWT auth and ownership-based permissions, built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and SQLAlchemy. Every endpoint is documented with Swagger.

**[Quorum](https://github.com/leelakrishnaRajasimha/Quorum)** — An AI "executive board" for startup due diligence. Agents play the roles of finance, market strategy, and product leads, and their assessments get combined into one investor-style report.

**[OpenMentor](https://github.com/leelakrishnaRajasimha/OpenMentor-Autonomous-Multi-Agent-Tutor)** — Four cooperating AI agents that look at a student's learning history, figure out what to study next, and build a revision timetable — with a peer-review step so agents catch each other's bad suggestions.

**[LogicSynth](https://github.com/leelakrishnaRajasimha/LogicSynth-Transformer)** — A decoder-only Transformer built from scratch, combining rotary positional embeddings with curriculum learning. Trained on 10-digit arithmetic, it generalizes to solve 15-digit problems at inference — no extra training needed.

**Gradient Monitor** — A lightweight training watchdog that tracks gradient norms and skips corrupted batches before they destabilize a run, adding only ~0.054ms of overhead per iteration. Tested it on physics datasets where bad batches were causing NaN losses.
